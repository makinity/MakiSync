# MakiSync — SMM Client Inquiry Form

**Project type:** Client onboarding / lead intake form
**Role:** Social Media Manager / Virtual Assistant
**Tool used:** Jotform
**Status:** Live

## Overview

A structured client inquiry form built to qualify and onboard new social media management leads. Designed to replace back-and-forth email threads with a single intake flow that captures everything needed to scope a proposal — business context, platform needs, goals, service tier interest, and existing brand assets — in one submission.

## Purpose

- Give potential clients a fast, guided way to describe their business and social media needs
- Pre-qualify leads by service tier and support type before the first call
- Collect brand assets upfront (logo, colors, fonts, guidelines) to shorten project ramp-up
- Present a professional, branded first touchpoint for the MakiSync SMM service

## Form Structure

| # | Field | Type | Required |
|---|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | Full Name | Short answer | Yes |
| 2 | Business / Brand Name | Short answer | Yes |
| 3 | Email Address | Short answer | Yes |
| 4 | Website / Social Media Page | Short answer | Yes |
| 5 | Tell me briefly about your business | Paragraph | Yes |
| 6 | Which social media platforms do you need help with? | Checkboxes (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Other) | Yes |
| 7 | What are your main social media goals? | Checkboxes (Brand awareness, Engagement, Followers, Leads, Sales, Online presence, Other) | Yes |
| 8 | Which service are you interested in? | Multiple choice (Part-Time $350/mo, Full-Time $800/mo, Hourly $5/hr, Not sure yet) | Yes |
| 9 | What kind of support do you need? | Checkboxes (Content planning, Content creation, Short-form video, Captions, Scheduling, Community management, Other) | Yes |
| 10 | Do you already have brand assets? | Multiple choice (Yes, Some, No) | Yes |
| 11 | Share your brand assets or brand kit | File upload / link | Optional |
| 12 | Anything else you'd like me to know? | Paragraph | Optional |

## Design Decisions

- **Service tiers surfaced early** (question 8) so leads self-select into a budget bracket before the discovery call, saving time on unqualified inquiries.
- **Goals and platforms separated** into distinct checkbox questions rather than one combined field, so overlap between "what they want" and "where they want it" doesn't get lost.
- **Brand asset upload kept optional** since not every lead has assets ready — but prompted early enough to shorten the eventual onboarding step.
- **Free-text field at the end** to catch anything the structured questions miss, without cluttering the required flow.
